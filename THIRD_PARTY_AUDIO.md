# Bundled offline speech

- Sherpa-onnx 1.13.7: Apache-2.0, https://github.com/k2-fsa/sherpa-onnx/tree/v1.13.7
- Kokoro multi-lang v1.0 int8: Apache-2.0 model; source and model card https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M ; converted release https://github.com/k2-fsa/sherpa-onnx/releases/tag/tts-models
- ONNX Runtime: MIT, https://github.com/microsoft/onnxruntime
- eSpeak NG engine/data: GPL-3.0-or-later; corresponding upstream source and license https://github.com/espeak-ng/espeak-ng . Sherpa integration/build sources are in its tagged repository above. Redistribution must preserve applicable notices and corresponding-source obligations; do not treat the complete bundle as solely Apache-licensed.

Pinned archive SHA-256 values and reproducible extraction are in scripts/prepare-android-speech.py. The APK includes the model LICENSE and espeak-ng data; this document records the other upstream notices and source locations. Voices: American af_heart (speaker 3, en-us) and British bf_emma (speaker 21, en). Speech is generated by a neural model, not a dictionary recording; homograph stress can require context. No user text is transmitted.
